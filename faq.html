Everything you need to know about THIHA's warehouse and logistics services.

General

THIHA Warehouses is a full-service warehouse and logistics provider offering storage solutions, logistics management, and inventory control services to businesses of all sizes. We operate a network of strategically located facilities and work closely with clients to ensure their goods are stored safely, managed accurately, and moved efficiently.
THIHA serves businesses across retail, e-commerce, and manufacturing — from growing startups to established enterprises. If your business holds, moves, or fulfils physical goods, THIHA can support your operations. Our flexible service model means we can tailor solutions regardless of your size or sector.
THIHA is located on Nairobi's Eastern Bypass, operated by Homeplus Realtors Limited. Contact us at 0722 375 143 or info@homepluskenya.com to discuss which services best suit your supply chain needs.

Storage

THIHA offers a range of storage configurations including pallet racking, bulk floor storage, shelving for smaller items, temperature-controlled storage, and secure high-value storage. We work with you to determine the most appropriate setup for your inventory type and volume.
Yes. THIHA's storage agreements are designed to be flexible. You can increase or reduce your allocated space as your inventory requirements change — whether that's accommodating seasonal peaks or scaling down during quieter periods. Speak to your account manager about the options available.
All THIHA facilities are protected by 24/7 CCTV monitoring, access control systems, and on-site security personnel. We also carry comprehensive goods-in-storage insurance. Specific security arrangements vary by facility — contact us for details relevant to your location.

Getting Started

The simplest way to get started is to book an online consultation. One of our team will get in touch to understand your requirements, walk you through the most appropriate service options, and provide a no-obligation quote. From there, we'll handle the onboarding process and get your goods into our system as efficiently as possible.
For most clients, THIHA can complete the onboarding process and be ready to receive your first inbound shipment within 5–10 business days of signing an agreement. Complex or high-volume setups may require additional time, which will be clearly communicated during your consultation.
THIHA offers both short-term and long-term agreements. Minimum terms vary depending on the service type and facility. Many clients start with a flexible month-to-month arrangement before moving to a longer-term contract once they're comfortable with the service. We'll outline the options clearly during your consultation.
